## 2. What the owner's decision changed (4.2)

Plainly: the structure lock (L2 / LOCKTIP / F2B) decided **no** item of the 900 under FROZEN, so removing it changed nothing directly. All 34 real reply changes come from removing one prompt line; 1 item is an F2B relabel with the same verdict. **Correct newly accepted: 1. Wrong newly accepted: 2 (A:110:m, A:842:m) — that is the cost.** Also 5 correct answers are newly rejected and 4 wrong answers newly rejected: net coverage 447 -> 443 (-4), net FA 21 -> 19 (-2).

**The surprise:** removing the "ALREADY VERIFIED" line made L3 *stricter*, not more lenient. The line told the model to "judge meaning and vocabulary only"; without it the model leans on the stored English reference, so on sentences with narrow or wrong references it now says DIFF where it said SAME. Owner's decision therefore bought a slightly lower FA at the price of slightly lower coverage on this set.

### Why TRANSLATION-ONLY accepts 4 fewer correct answers (stage 5's surprise)

The TRANSLATION-ONLY request differs from FROZEN by exactly one user-prompt line (test_2i (f)): "Practised grammar: <topic> - ALREADY VERIFIED as correct in this answer; judge meaning and vocabulary only." Same model, temperature 0, same system text. So every reply change below is caused by that line. Yes - the line made FROZEN's L3 lenient: "judge meaning and vocabulary only" told it to look past form, and with it gone the model leans on the English reference. All 5 correct answers it now rejects sit on sentences whose reference is narrow or wrong (pro-drop gender fixed to "she", an invented "her brother", "fetch" for nosila, "loves the burning"). The same leniency ran the other way on grammar: of the wrong answers both stacks reject, grammar slips moved TIP -> DIFF.
