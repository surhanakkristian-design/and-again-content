# Phase 1T — fresh Slovak set, WRITER SPEC (you are a blind writer: do NOT open any other file or directory of this repo)

You write test material for a checker of learner translations Slovak → English (learner sees the Slovak sentence, types English). You write NEW Slovak sentences, 4 CORRECT and 5 WRONG learner answers for each, and a small annotation. Invent everything yourself; do not read earlier sets. Natural, everyday, idiomatic Slovak with full diacritics; natural learner-like English (British or American, be consistent within a sentence).

## The owner's rules (verbatim) — what CORRECT and WRONG mean
ACCEPTANCE RULE: "the reference point is the SLOVAK sentence, not the English reference. If the learner's sentence is correct English on its own AND means what the Slovak means, it is ACCEPTED with points, even if it avoids the practised structure entirely. Learners often do not know which structure is being practised and must not be penalised for that. The practised structure becomes a TIP, not a gate."
TENSE RULE: LEVEL 1, the TIME FRAME (past / present / future) must match the Slovak — a frame shift is WRONG. LEVEL 2, the choice of English tense WITHIN that frame is FREE where the Slovak does not fix it (Slovak past imperfective `On trénoval hodiny` admits "was training", "trained" and "had been training" alike) — CORRECT.
OMISSION RULE: "M1, a dropped FUNCTION word or particle (just, already, an article, optional "that") — CORRECT, accepted, the missing word surfaced as a tip. M2, a dropped CONTENT word (noun, main verb, meaning-carrying adjective or adverb) — WRONG. The app teaches translation, not gist. M3, ADDED content — WRONG."
AGENT RULE: "A translation is judged only on whether it renders the whole Slovak sentence. An English passive is CORRECT when it keeps the agent (e.g. 'is repaired by my father'), or when the Slovak sentence itself names no agent. When the Slovak names an agent (a nominative subject doing the action, including a pronoun or 'niekto') and the English answer drops it, the learner left out half the translation: that is an omission and the answer is WRONG. Dropping any other content word is WRONG too; dropping only a function word or particle is CORRECT."
Error types for WRONG answers: T = time frame shifted (past/present/future differs from the Slovak) · W = wrong word (a lexical substitution changing which thing, person, place, time or quantity) · M = meaning added or dropped · S = small slip (article, preposition, agreement, word form/spelling: wrong English or slightly changed meaning).

## Arm-B convention for the Slovak (mandatory)
An explicit subject personal pronoun (ja / ty / on / ona / my / vy / oni) wherever Slovak would normally drop the subject; sentences with a noun subject stay as they are; a genuinely impersonal, subjectless, passive or reflexive-passive Slovak sentence gets NO added subject. Applies to EVERY finite clause, also embedded ones ("On povedal, že ona opraví…").

## Sentence mix — 25 sentences per writer (your level, SID range and topic domains are in your task message)
- 5 sentences of kind SKP: the Slovak ITSELF names no agent — a true passive (bol postavený, je zatvorené), a reflexive passive (Dom sa stavia už dva roky. Tu sa predáva chlieb.) or a subjectless/impersonal sentence (Včera nám ukradli bicykel is NOT allowed — 3rd-plural impersonal is ambiguous; use Prší / Je tu zima / Treba to opraviť / Hovorí sa, že… types sparingly; prefer passive and reflexive passive). Mix the three.
- 20 sentences of kind ACT: an explicit nominative agent (noun, proper name, or arm-B pronoun; at least 6 of the 20 with a pronoun agent) doing a TRANSITIVE action that English can naturally passivize (locks the gate, repaired the car, will deliver the parcel).
  - Of the 20 ACT sentences, the number given in your task message as EMB must ALSO contain an EMBEDDED (subordinate) clause that has its own explicit nominative agent and its own passivizable transitive verb: že-clauses, keď/keďže/pretože/lebo/hoci/kým/ak/aby clauses, relative clauses (ktorý/ktorá/ktoré…); vary the type and put the subordinate clause first in about a third of them. Example: "Ona povedala, že sused zajtra opraví ten plot." / "Keď my upraceme kuchyňu, on umyje riad." The main clause should also be passivizable where natural.
- Vary the time frame: about 40 % past, 35 % present, 25 % future, and vary grammar topics appropriate to the level. No two sentences about the same event; no sentence longer than 22 Slovak words; A1 sentences ≤ 9 words.

## Answers — exactly 4 correct (aids c1–c4) and 5 wrong (aids w1–w5) per sentence, all DIFFERENT strings
CORRECT, ACT sentence: c1 plain faithful translation · c2 another faithful translation differing by a determiner choice (the/that/my where Slovak allows it) or by a free within-frame tense choice · c3 a BY-PASSIVE that keeps the agent ("The gate is locked by Marek every evening.") — in EMB sentences make the by-passive in the EMBEDDED clause for every second sentence, in the main clause otherwise · c4 a faithful paraphrase (word order, synonym, contraction). Never write an agent-dropping passive as a correct answer.
CORRECT, SKP sentence: at least 3 of the 4 are English passives or otherwise agentless renderings that are fully faithful ("Bread is sold here." "The house has been under construction for two years." is fine too); everything in the Slovak must be rendered.
WRONG, ACT sentence:
 - w1 type T: a clear TIME-FRAME shift, everything else right (not a within-frame tense change!).
 - w2 type M, tag agentdrop-main: grammatical English passive of the MAIN clause with the agent left out, everything else (time, place, objects, the whole embedded clause if any) fully kept. "The garden gate is locked every evening."
 - w3: in an EMB sentence type M, tag agentdrop-embedded: the MAIN clause is translated faithfully WITH its agent, the EMBEDDED clause is turned into an agentless passive that drops only the embedded agent ("She said that the fence would be repaired tomorrow."). In a non-EMB sentence: type W.
 - w4: type M other than an agent drop (a content adjective, a place or time phrase dropped — or a content word added), alternating with type W from sentence to sentence. In the sentences listed in your task message as EMB2, w4 is instead a SECOND agentdrop-embedded item built differently from w3 (get-passive, different tense-within-frame, or different word order) — still dropping only the embedded agent.
 - w5 type S: a small slip that is wrong English (agreement, preposition, word form, missing obligatory article), meaning intact.
WRONG, SKP sentence: w1 T, w2 T (a different frame than w1, or the same frame shift built differently), w3 W, w4 M (content dropped or added), w5 S.
Each wrong answer must be wrong for EXACTLY ONE reason and otherwise perfect; each must be something a real learner would plausibly type. A possessive must not smuggle the agent back in ("My bike was sold" for *Ja som predal bicykel* — avoid such constructions in agent-drop items: the agent must be absent altogether, and no leftover pronoun referring to it anywhere in that clause).
