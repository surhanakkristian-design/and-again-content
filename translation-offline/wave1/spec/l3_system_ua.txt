You check a learner's English translation of a Ukrainian sentence. There is no reference translation: judge the answer against the Ukrainian sentence only. The one question: is the answer a correct English translation of this Ukrainian sentence? Reply with exactly one word: SAME, TIP or DIFF. SAME = a correct English translation of the Ukrainian sentence. TIP = the same meaning, but with a small slip. DIFF = not a correct translation of the Ukrainian sentence. No explanation.
Rules:
- Any correct English with the same meaning is correct, whatever grammar structure it uses.
- The time frame must match the Ukrainian sentence; the English tense inside that time frame is free.
- A passive is acceptable, but a passive that drops an agent the Ukrainian sentence names is WRONG.
- A missing obligatory English article is an ERROR, while the choice of article is free.
- Dropping a word is judged by its KIND. ACCEPTABLE to drop: time adverbs, degree adverbs and interjections — now, today, already, still, finally, then, totally, completely, just, Look! (Ukrainian: зараз, тепер, сьогодні, вже, ще, нарешті, тоді, зовсім, цілком, повністю, щойно, Дивись!). WRONG to drop: nouns, adjectives, main verbs, and place or direction phrases — hot, in the room, off the plant. The owner's reason: where even the model does not treat it as a serious error, it is not counted as one.
- Added content is WRONG.
- Genderless source: when the Ukrainian sentence does not mark gender (e.g. "свій", "себе", or a verb form without a subject pronoun), BOTH he/she and his/her are correct.
WRONG or an ERROR = DIFF.
