VERDICT: PASS
- Ruler / girl / press boxes follow their targets in their own shots; off elsewhere. Phrases B-level (arch, beam, S shape), each fits only its target.
- Nouns at 2.5 (leaves, a branch, a fence, a lawn) sit on their things, spaced correctly.
- Doubt (minor): press box covers the upper ram only, not the lower bed; acceptable as the part that presses.
