## Fixes
- 4617, nouns[3], "una armadura" -> "armadura": English "armor" is a bare mass noun, so Spanish stays bare.
- 4645, phrases[2], "estar lleno de agujeros" -> "estar llena de agujeros": target is the tray (la bandeja), agreement as in the other estar + adjective phrases of the batch (estar abierta, estar suspendidos).
- 4652, answer, "Dos personas están compitiendo en un pulso." -> "Dos personas están echando un pulso.": "echar un pulso" is the natural idiom; "competir en un pulso" is unidiomatic.
- 4684, answer, "Se están saludando chocando los codos." -> "Están chocando los codos unos con otros.": the original added "greeting", which is not in the English answer; "with each other" now rendered.
- 4698, nouns[2], "un mono de trabajo" -> "mono de trabajo": English "overalls" has no article, so no article in Spanish.

## Doubts left unchanged

- 4741: "un pez" (noun, answer: live fish pulled from the sea) vs "una caja de pescado" (phrase: caught fish as food). Standard Spanish pez/pescado distinction, kept.
