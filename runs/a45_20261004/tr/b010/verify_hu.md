Texts checked: 890 (100 videos: 300 phrases, 390 nouns, 100 questions, 100 answers).

## Fixes (7)
- 850, phrases[0]: "az ég felé emelkedni" -> "az égbe emelkedni". English is "into the sky", not "towards the sky".
- 882, phrases[0]: "edzést végezni" -> "edzeni". "Edzést végez" is officialese; the natural Hungarian verb is "edz".
- 882, answer: "Edzést végez." -> "Éppen edz." Same verb as the phrase; "éppen" marks the ongoing action and avoids a one-word sentence.
- 4035, phrases[1]: "a toronyról lógni" -> "a toronyról lelógni". With the delative "-ról" Hungarian needs "lelóg" (hang down from); "lóg" takes "-n".
- 4044, phrases[0]: "a golfot gyakorolni" -> "a golfozást gyakorolni". One practises the activity ("golfozás"); "a golfot gyakorolni" is unidiomatic.
- 4044, answer: "Éjszaka a golfot gyakorolja." -> "Éjszaka a golfozást gyakorolja." Same word as the phrase.
- 4047, phrases[1]: "felvenni a napszemüveget" -> "napszemüveget felvenni". English has no definite article ("to put on sunglasses"); bare object as in the other entries.
