Texts checked: 890 (100 videos: 300 phrases, 390 nouns, 100 questions, 100 answers).

## Fixes (4)
- 841, phrases[1]: "topiť sa v kanvici" -> "roztápať sa v kanvici". Same verb as the answer of the same video ("Roztápa ľad v kanvici"); "topiť sa" also reads as "to drown".
- 4031, nouns[2]: "sieť" -> "sieťka". The net of a table-tennis table is "sieťka" in Slovak; "sieť" is the wrong sense.
- 4059, phrases[1]: "vtlačiť sa medzi ostatných" -> "vtlačiť sa medzi ostatné". The others are baboons (animal masculine, plural declines as non-personal), so the accusative is "ostatné".
- 4059, question: "Na čo uprene hľadia tí traja paviáni?" -> "Na čo uprene hľadia tie tri paviány?". Animal masculine nouns take the non-personal plural: "tie tri paviány".
