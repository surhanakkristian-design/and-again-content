# b018 de verification

Texts checked: 891 (100 videos: phrases, nouns, question, answer)

## Fixes
- 4974 phrases[1]: Tassen Tee verteilen -> Becher mit Tee verteilen (the cups are the steel cups of the nouns (Stahlbecher); same word for the same thing)
- 4978 answer: Er kocht Reis in einer Pfanne. -> Er brät Reis in einer Pfanne. (fried rice tossed in a wok; "kochen" reads as boiling)
- 5032 phrases[1]: Schnee auf dem Gipfel haben -> Schnee auf den Gipfeln haben (subject is plural (the snowy mountains))
- 5089 question: Was macht die Mutter? -> Was backt die Mutter? ("Was macht ...?" means "what is she doing", not "what is she making"; matches the answer verb backen)

- 4977: "Neben wem sitzt der Mann?" for a monkey; acceptable for animals.
