## Fixes (4)
- 359, phrases[1]: "manger quelques graines" -> "manger des graines". "some seeds" is the partitive/indefinite "des", not "a few"; now matches the answer ("Il mange des graines dans un bol.").
- 397, phrases[0]: "scruter à travers des jumelles" -> "regarder à travers des jumelles". "scruter" is transitive and needs an object; without one the phrase is ungrammatical.
- 414, question: "Que fait la femme devant ?" -> "Que fait la femme de devant ?". "devant" read as an adverb of the verb ("what is the woman doing in front?"); "de devant" identifies the woman in front.
- 423, phrases[0]: "souffler un baiser" -> "envoyer un baiser". Calque of "blow a kiss"; the French expression is "envoyer un baiser".
