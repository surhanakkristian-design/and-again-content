## Fixes (9)
- 308, phrases[2]: "dívat se na ně nahoru" -> "dívat se k nim nahoru" — unnatural combination; direction upwards takes "k nim nahoru".
- 310, question: "Kde jdou?" -> "Kudy jdou?" — with a verb of motion and the answer "Jdou lesem." the question word is "kudy".
- 311, phrases[0]: "držet vidličku nahoře" -> "držet zdviženou vidličku" — natural wording for "hold up".
- 312, answer: "Píská na píšťalku faul." -> "Píská na píšťalku kvůli faulu." — two objects on "pískat" is ungrammatical; "for a foul" = "kvůli faulu".
- 352, phrases[1]: "držet nahoře jeden prst" -> "držet jeden zdvižený prst" — natural wording for "hold up", same pattern as 311.
- 355, answer: "Boří obličej do dlaní." -> "Zabořuje obličej do dlaní." — "bořit" reads as "demolish"; the imperfective of the phrase verb "zabořit" is "zabořovat".
- 372, phrases[2]: "točit se vzduchem" -> "točit se ve vzduchu" — the bare instrumental does not work with "točit se".
- 389, nouns[2]: "chleba" -> "chléb" — dictionary nominative form; the accusative "chleba" in the phrases and answer stays.
- 418, phrases[0]: "vypáčit kroužek" -> "rozevřít kroužek" — "vypáčit" means to force out or break open (a door, a lock); opening a key ring is "rozevřít".
