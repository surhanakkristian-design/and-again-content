## Rules and decisions a new session needs
- Brief rules: Opus for the main session and ALL content (writers, verifiers, translators, grammar verifiers; they look at the frames); mechanical work by scripts. No Gemini, no paid service, free local tools only. Never print a key. Browser checks muted. Nothing in the chat while working; at the end one line with the report name. CLAUDE.md "Speed and safety of runs": every command with a timeout (at most 10 minutes, longer ones in the background), parallel subagents.
- Database: the classifier refused the live migration ("Production Deploy"). Do not retry and do not look for another way; read-only queries and rolled-back dry runs worked. All writes and uploads go through the ONE owner script `apply_a45.sh`. Storage: new object names only. Before appending to the decision log (`translation-offline/upload_skcz/OWNER_DECISIONS_2L.md`): `git pull`, next free number (A45's are 357-359).
- Content rules: `RUN/WRITER_BRIEF.md`, `RUN/VERIFIER_BRIEF.md`, `RUN/TR_BRIEF.md`, `RUN/TR_VERIFY_BRIEF.md` (do not change them in the middle of the run without a reason; they are what 1,200 videos were made with). Level: A = simple everyday words; B = at least one B1/B2 word or collocation per phrase and in the answer. Videos with one or two targets are allowed. Voice per item: target / subject gender, else the video's default (main person's gender, else even id female, odd id male).
- A video whose content fails verification twice is listed and skipped (none so far). `batch.py finish` puts every video without a PASS / FIXED verdict, or with a validation error, into `data/<batch>.result.json` `failed` with the reason.
- Agents share the folders: every helper file an agent makes must carry one of its media ids (or batch + language) in its name. In b001 writers overwrote each other's `gen.py` and re-ran foreign scripts. `batch.py` marks a content file changed after its verdict as `STALE` (verify it again).
- 20 parallel Agent-tool subagents is the session limit; the Workflow tool (16 at once per workflow) ran two workflows side by side without trouble. One group of 4 videos = about 175 k tokens for the writer and 150 k for the verifier; a batch of 100 = about 9 M tokens for writing + verifying and 1.5 M for the translations.
- Audio under load: `say` sometimes hangs; `tts.py` retries 3 times with 4 threads. Run `batch.py finish` in the background with a 30-minute limit and run it again if videos fail with "audio: no manifest" (it only records what is missing).
- Known content doubts to keep for the final report: media 74 ("bat"): the description says a fruit bat in a cave, the frames show a woman with a baseball bat; the content follows the frames. Some phrases are states ("to wear ...", "to have long hair") where two people do the same thing. Verifier notes per video: `RUN/verify/<id>.md`; translation fixes per batch and language: `RUN/tr/<batch>/verify_<code>.md`.
