# Pre-check word 8055 (balloon = hot-air balloon) - A51, free, no credits spent

Still: a49 stills/8055.png, a black-ink pen drawing on white paper. A striped hot-air balloon with dark and white gores, a light scalloped band, a dark bottom band and a small mouth sits in the upper half. Three small hatched clouds surround it. Long ropes hang from the mouth to the lower middle, where a small man in a light suit and tie hangs from them by one raised arm, his face turned right. **The still has NO basket, no ground and no burner.** It has no logos or lettering. The drafts are backed up in /private/tmp/claude-501/a51_8055/drafts/.

Applies to all three:
- The drafts only said "the man no longer hangs". An edit model usually adds the new man and leaves the old hanging man and the long ropes in place, which gives a duplicate. Every prompt now says the long ropes and the hanging man are erased completely, says "the one and only man", and lists "a second man" under Must NOT appear.
- The keep-block now says the envelope is not mirrored and keeps the same stripes.
- The basket has to be invented, and invented wicker often gets a sponsor name. Every prompt now says the envelope and basket are completely plain, with no lettering, emblem, number or flag.
- The man's face stays a blank oval, so his eyeline is given as a head turn.

## to fly in a balloon
Before: The basket was not placed, so it could have landed at the end of the long ropes, far below the balloon. "Small landscape at the bottom" could have come out as near ground, which reads as landing. Hands and eyeline were vague. A flame or bird could have cluttered the picture.
Changed: The ropes and the hanging man are erased. A plain basket as wide as the mouth hangs on four short ropes right under it. The man is inside, seen from the waist up, with one hand on the rim and the other waving with an open palm, his head turned down. The landscape is a thin strip of tiny fields, a river and tree dots at the very bottom, no taller than a tenth of the page. A wide band of empty air separates it from the basket. Must NOT appear now covers the land-sibling cues (ground contact, ropes held from the ground, sagging envelope) and the basket-sibling cue (a big close-up basket).
Remaining risk: medium. The framing is kept, so the man in the basket is small and the waving hand may be only a few pixels at thumbnail size. "Flying" still reads from the balloon, the basket and the far land. The tiny roofs could pick up sign-like marks, which would be a text hard fail, so check them closely.

## to land a balloon
Before: The draft did not say the balloon moves down, so the model could have kept it high and added a separate ground below, or left the old man hanging. "Half deflated" could have been drawn as a burst or torn envelope, or the basket tipped over, which reads as a crash (scary). The crew had no features, which risks clones of the man, and their ropes and eyelines were undefined.
Changed: The bottom fifth is now a meadow. The basket stands upright with its bottom pressed into the grass and the tufts flattened. The envelope is lower (its top no higher than the middle of the page), soft, leaning right with folds, and "not torn, no hole". Two fictional crew are described (a woman in her thirties with a ponytail, a man in his fifties with a grey beard). Each pulls a rope with both hands up to the envelope and looks up at it. The passenger holds the rim and looks down. Must NOT appear now lists burst, smoke, fire, crash, tipped basket, falling, waving, far landscape and empty air under the basket.
Remaining risk: medium. This is the biggest layout change from the still, because the balloon has to move down. The model may keep the full round envelope and only add the ground, which is still readable but weaker. The rope-pulling hands are an anatomy risk. A half-collapsed envelope may smear the stripe pattern.

## balloon basket
Before: "Close-up ... filling the frame" was not concrete enough for an edit model, which tends to keep the whole balloon at the same size and only add a small basket. Without the burner and the envelope's mouth, a lone basket could read as a picnic or laundry basket. The old hanging man and the clouds had nowhere to go in a close-up. A man inside the basket would read as the flying picture.
Changed: The camera moves much closer and lower. The basket fills the lower half at about three quarters of the page width. It has woven hatching, a padded rim, rope handles, plain sandbags, and a burner frame of four poles with a double burner and no flame. Cables run into the envelope's mouth, cut off by the top edge. The clouds are hidden and the basket is empty. The one man stands beside it on the grass, smaller, with his hand on the rim, looking at it. Must NOT appear lists picnic/laundry basket, top handle, a whole balloon in the sky, people holding ropes, a deflating envelope and a flame.
Remaining risk: medium. The edit model may refuse the large camera move and give a mid-shot of the whole balloon standing on the ground, which brings it close to "to land a balloon". The man beside the basket adds to that risk. The burner frame may come out as a messy tangle of lines. Sandbags may get printed marks, which would be a text hard fail, so check them closely.
